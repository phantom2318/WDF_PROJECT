Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access my student dashboard?

Select Login and enter your registered student ID and password.

How can I create a new account?

Select Register to complete the student registration form and create your account.

What can I manage in the portal?

You can manage academic information, fees, clubs, events, profile details, and registrations.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Contact your institution's portal administrator for help restoring access.

Where can I check my fee information?

Log in and open the Fees section from your student dashboard to view your payment details.

How do I join a club or event?

Open Clubs or Extracurricular from the dashboard, choose an available activity, and submit your registration.

Can I update my profile information?

Yes. Open your Profile page after logging in to review and update the information available to you.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

Use the theme toggle available in the portal settings to change the appearance of the portal.

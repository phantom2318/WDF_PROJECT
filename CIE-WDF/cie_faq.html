How can I register for an event?

Fill the form provided by the Event host.You must save the details of the form for your own safety.

Is event registration free?

Events conducted by university are free.Events conducted by Clubs are paid.

Where will the event be conducted?

Can I participate in more than one event?

If the time of time of multiple events does not collide with each other,then Participation in multiple events can be done.

It is upto student to manage the time of multiple events.
